Welcome to Kitsuvo

Choose what Kitsuvo does in your searches. Both settings are optional and off until you turn them on.

On search pages

Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Bing Copilot answers and Duck.ai. Read from the page only. You can show any hidden answer on the page.

Google.com, Bing, and DuckDuckGo. Uses known builder hosting only, without visiting results. You can reveal hidden results on the page. Reload search tabs if needed.

Each setting asks for access to the supported search sites only when you turn it on. Access is released when both are off.

With a setting on, a short line above the results says what Kitsuvo checked or hid. You can hide it on the page and bring it back from the popup.

No page data leaves your browser.

Scan this page

Open the Kitsuvo popup from the toolbar and choose Scan this page to see local builder evidence for the open tab. Scores are clues, not proof of authorship or safety.

Change these later

Click the Kitsuvo icon in the toolbar to open the popup. It has the same settings, Help to replay the guide and a Setup guide link back to this page. If the icon is hidden, pin Kitsuvo from your browser’s extensions menu.
